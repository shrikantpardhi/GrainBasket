# Phase 1 store application specification

**Platform:** Android, Kotlin with Jetpack Compose, for the one Nagpur store. This is a separate staff app using the same authorized Node.js backend and database as the [consumer](consumer.md) and [admin](admin.md) apps; staff never connect to PostgreSQL directly.

## Staff flow

| Step | Required behavior | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access and language | Sign in with an admin-created staff account; choose English, Hindi or Marathi. | Only active staff for this store can enter. All order actions and status labels work in each language; changing language does not change saved order values. |
| Queue | Refresh and see consumer orders with verified online payment and staff-created orders with verified online payment or cash collected at entry. Show order number, source, item choices, amount paid, pickup/delivery details and store opening/closing times. For flour pickup, show **grind on arrival** or **ready at selected time**, requested time or arrival, current estimated ready time and delay; order by the time staff need to prepare/serve them. | Unverified online payments and unpaid cash orders never appear as actionable orders. Staff see only their store's orders. The time shown is an estimate until staff mark `READY_FOR_PICKUP`; no offline status changes are queued. |
| Place order for a customer | Staff can build an order in the store app without the consumer app: select flour/rice variants, grind if flour, quantity, pickup/delivery and required customer name/mobile; collect a delivery address if needed. Ask separately whether the customer wants marketing messages; consent defaults to no and can be withdrawn on request. For flour pickup, record the preparation choice and requested pickup time where applicable. Server confirms stock, fees, time availability and total before staff submit. | No customer app account or OTP is required for staff order entry. Staff identity and customer contact snapshot are recorded; an unverified phone number is never silently linked to an existing customer account. Declining marketing does not block checkout or rewards. Unsupported pincodes, unavailable times and stale price/stock block submission. |
| Rewards | If the customer wants to use points, verify their mobile by OTP in the store app, show the available balance and minimum, and apply any eligible points in the server quote before cash collection or online payment. An unverified contact may complete an order without points and later claim its earned points after phone verification. | No points are redeemed from a typed phone number alone. Staff cannot edit a points balance or change the quoted total. The same order never awards points twice, even after retry or refresh. |
| Take payment | Staff select cash or online for their entered order. For cash, **collect it before submitting** and record staff actor and amount in the same server operation as order creation. For online payment, provide a provider-hosted customer payment flow without entering card details in the store app. | Staff cannot set an online transaction to `PAID`; only backend verification can. No cash order is placed without staff confirming collection. A repeat submit cannot create duplicate charges/orders. Cash collections and manual refunds are visible to admin for reconciliation. |
| Pending online orders | Show staff-entered online orders awaiting verification separately from the fulfillment queue, with their payment expiry/failure state. | Staff can check the server outcome after an uncertain connection without creating a second order or assuming the customer's payment succeeded. |
| Accept or cancel | Accept a new order, or cancel a paid order before handover with a reason. Consumer-app customers see the status on refresh; staff contact customers whose orders they entered themselves. | Duplicate/invalid transitions are rejected by the server. Cancelling a paid order initiates the tracked refund workflow; prepared stock is not automatically returned as sellable. |
| Prepare | For in-front pickup, record customer arrival and start milling only after arrival and acceptance; show the 30-minute baseline wait and a current ready estimate. For advance pickup, schedule milling after acceptance so the whole order is ready for the selected time. Update the estimate with a reason if delayed; pack and mark ready only after preparation. | Grind and rice-age choices remain visible. Arrival, estimate changes, preparation and ready timestamps record staff actor/time. An updated estimate appears in the consumer order on refresh where applicable; staff contact customers they entered themselves. |
| Handover | For pickup mark collected; for delivery mark out for delivery and then delivered. Delivery coordination happens outside the app. | Cannot finish the wrong fulfillment branch, hand over a cancelled order or mark an unpaid order completed. Failed delivery is resolved by staff cancellation with reason and refund review. |
| Stock | View and adjust available stock of sellable flour weights and rice age/weight variants; give a reason for a manual correction. | Stock never goes below zero or consumes stock reserved for paid orders; only admin changes catalog/price/translation. |

The store app needs online access for changes. If the network fails, show the last confirmed state and ask staff to retry or refresh rather than assuming a transition succeeded. [Operations](operations.md) defines the shared state machine and payment/refund handling.

## Release checks

In each language, staff handle both flour pickup modes: for in-front grinding, check in the customer before milling and update the ready estimate as queue work changes; for advance pickup, prepare before the requested time. Check an unavailable time, a delay, and a mixed flour/rice pickup. Repeat for rice delivery. Place a walk-in cash order and a staff-entered online order without the consumer app, checking name/mobile, optional marketing consent, points verification/redemption, server total, payment recording and duplicate-submit handling. Check cancellation/refund visibility, invalid transition, manual stock correction, failed network write and attempted access to an order outside the assigned store.
