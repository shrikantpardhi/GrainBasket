# Phase 1 consumer application specification

**Platform:** Android, Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. This app follows the [Phase 1 business scope](phase-1.md); common order/payment and data rules are in [Operations](operations.md). It uses the shared Node.js backend API, not a direct database connection.

## Customer flow

| Step | Required behavior | Acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Language | Choose English, Hindi or Marathi at first use or in settings. Persist the choice; show localized UI labels, product/category text and status labels. | All three languages allow the full checkout journey. Missing catalog translation falls back to English without hiding a product; prices and identifiers never change with language. |
| Browse | Guest can browse active flour/rice and see images, weight, price, stock availability, grind choice for flour and verified rice age for rice. | Inactive/unavailable variants cannot be ordered. A flour line requires fine/medium/coarse; different grinds remain separate cart lines. |
| Sign in and contact | Ask for customer name and verify mobile number by OTP at checkout. Offer a separate optional marketing opt-in; show it unchecked by default and allow withdrawal in settings. | Invalid, expired or reused OTP fails; retry limits apply. Guest browsing does not require login. Order contact details do not imply permission to send marketing. |
| Fulfillment | Choose store pickup (show location, configured opening/closing times and closed days) or delivery (enter recipient name, phone, address and supported pincode). For flour pickup, choose **grind in front of me** or **ready at a selected pickup time**. | In-front grinding displays an approximately 30-minute wait, with its start defined in [Operations](operations.md). For ready-in-advance, show available times within open hours before payment and record the selected time. Delivery flour is prepared before dispatch. Unsupported pincode or closed ordering window blocks checkout. |
| Quote, points and pay | Show verified points balance and allow redemption only when the configured minimum is reached. Cart displays estimated totals; server confirms availability, any points applied, item prices, any required tax breakdown, delivery fee and payable total. Customer explicitly confirms and starts UPI or card payment for the remaining amount. | A changed balance/price/stock requires reconfirmation. Server prevents double spending and never charges more than the confirmed total. Never collect card details in GrainBasket. Repeat taps/retries cannot create duplicate paid orders or charges. |
| Payment outcome | Show pending while awaiting server verification, then paid order number or failure/expiry with retry option. | A client-side success screen alone never marks an order paid. Failed/expired attempt does not enter the store queue; reserved stock is released. If an outcome is uncertain, show pending and let the customer check status without paying blindly again. |
| Order history and rewards | Show own paid orders with item choices, amount, payment status, current fulfillment status, cancellation reason and refund progress; refresh online. For flour pickup, show preparation choice and requested time or arrival-based wait, plus the current estimated ready time and any delay. Show points earned on completed orders, points used and current balance. | Customer cannot view another person's order or balance. Do not claim flour is ready until the store marks it ready; show changed estimates rather than the original time as a guarantee. No points are earned from pending, cancelled or failed orders; a cancelled paid order shows refund pending/completed rather than suggesting cash was returned immediately. |

Offline browsing cache and push notifications are optional later. In Phase 1, failed connectivity shows a retry state and order status is refreshed online; never claim an order was submitted when its result is unknown.

## Release checks

Complete both flour pickup choices and the delivery journey in each of the three languages. Check that the order displays a requested/estimated ready time, staff changes to the estimate appear on refresh, and ready status is shown only after preparation. Verify points accrue once at completion and minimum-balance redemption changes the confirmed payment total. Test invalid OTP, marketing opt-out, unsupported delivery pincode, unavailable pickup time, stale price, sold-out variant, repeated checkout, declined payment, delayed provider confirmation, cancellation/refund, and unauthorized access to another customer's order or balance. Use the same backend prices and statuses in every language.
